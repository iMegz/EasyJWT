            Don't have the key at all? Recover it from two tokens
            
Needs two different tokens signed with the same RS256/RS384/RS512 key (assumes the near-universal exponent 65537; won't work on PS256/384/512). This does a real factorization step and can take anywhere from several seconds to a minute or more per key size tried, the page will be unresponsive while it works.
